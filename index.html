FEATURED PROJECT / DIMORA

Your floor plan.
A home you can explore.

Created and developed by me. A home-planning application that turns 2D floor plans into interactive 3D spaces.

2D → 3DInteractive interiorsHome planning
What you can do +

Upload a floor plan and generate a 3D home. Rotate, zoom, and walk through rooms; edit layouts, arrange furniture, and change wall colours, flooring, and themes. View room measurements and estimate interior costs.

Why I built it +

To help people understand a home beyond its floor plan—and explore design choices before making real-world decisions.
